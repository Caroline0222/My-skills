# Email Master

Cross-border business email drafting and rewriting for client, vendor, and internal team communications—in **English or Chinese**.

Describe the context, and get a ready-to-send email calibrated to your recipient and objective:

- **Recipient-Aligned Tone:** Adapts register and structure across external clients, senior leadership, vendors, and cross-functional teams.
- **Structured & Diplomatic:** Provides clear frameworks when holding scope or timeline boundaries, and measured phrasing when negotiating trade-offs.
- **Complete Send-Out Package:** Delivers **three subject line options**, a **ready-to-send body**, a **brief IM follow-up note**, and a **pre-send checklist**.

## Usage

```bash
cp -r skills/email-master ~/.workbuddy/skills/
```

Describe your scenario directly:

- *"A client is requesting new features during UAT while keeping next Friday's go-live date."*
- *"Communicate a 5-day launch delay to an executive with business impact and mitigation options."*
- *"Send a formal reminder to a vendor after two missed deadlines."*
- *"Summarize today's cross-border meeting into clear decisions and action items."*
